Use a flexible tape, keep it level, and don’t pull tight. Enter full chest, waist and hip circumferences.

If the size chart isn’t visible, open the store’s “Size guide.” MeasureLive reads text tables, including many charts inside popups. It cannot reliably read every image, widget or language.

Check whether the chart lists body measurements or garment measurements. Garment sizes need extra room and depend on fabric, so they do not produce an automatic recommendation.
